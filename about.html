About

The short version

Mathematics student and researcher based in Bangladesh, working across pure theory, computation, and teaching.

I'm a mathematics student with an active research practice that spans fractional stochastic differential equations and nonlinear wave dynamics — and their computational side, the part where an elegant equation has to survive contact with Maple, MATLAB, and Python.

That same computational instinct carries into applied work: modelling flood dynamics in Dinajpur with a fractional stochastic PDE, and mapping land use change across Gazipur District using satellite imagery and machine learning. I like problems that force theory to meet real, noisy data.

Alongside research, I tutor mathematics and spend a fair amount of time teaching myself things adjacent to my field — quantum mechanics and computing, operations research — usually by writing the explanation I wish I'd had, in Bengali, with the English terms kept intact for later reading.
